Seasonal Design Bundles: Plan Your Holiday Craft Calendar

📅 September 25, 2025⏱ 6 min read

Seasonal designs drive some of the biggest sales peaks for Etsy sellers and craft businesses. Halloween, Christmas, Thanksgiving, Valentine's Day — each holiday brings a surge of shoppers looking for themed products. The key to capitalizing on these peaks is to buy your design bundles early and prepare your products in advance.

Your Seasonal Design Calendar

  • August–September: Halloween and fall/autumn designs
  • September–November: Thanksgiving designs
  • October–December: Christmas and holiday designs
  • January: Valentine's Day prep begins
  • February–March: Easter and spring designs
  • May–June: Summer, graduation and Father's Day

Halloween Designs: A Hot Market

Halloween is one of the biggest seasons for craft sellers. Spooky SVGs, Halloween sublimation designs and seasonal clipart all sell exceptionally well from August through October. The Spooky Training & Learning SVG Bundle is perfect for teachers and classroom-themed Halloween items.

Christmas: The Biggest Sales Season

Christmas is the undisputed king of seasonal craft sales. With over 150 designs, the Bestseller Mega Christmas Bundle gives you everything you need to stock your shop with holiday-ready designs. Start preparing your Christmas products by October to catch early holiday shoppers.

Thanksgiving: Don't Miss This Opportunity

Thanksgiving is often overlooked by sellers who focus entirely on Christmas. But Thanksgiving-themed mugs, shirts, signs and home decor sell very well in October and November. The Thanksgiving Quotes Designs Bundle with 263+ designs gives you unbeatable variety.

Pro Tip: Bundle Your Seasonal Products

Create product bundles (sets of 3–5 related items) in your Etsy shop around each holiday. Customers love the value of getting multiple coordinated items, and bundles help increase your average order value. Use a variety of designs from your bundles to create cohesive product sets.
