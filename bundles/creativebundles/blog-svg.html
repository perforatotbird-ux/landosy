SVG Bundles Explained: How to Choose the Right One for Your Project

📅 October 1, 2025⏱ 6 min read

SVG bundles are one of the most popular types of digital download products — and for good reason. Whether you're using a Cricut Maker, Silhouette Cameo, or any other cutting machine, SVG files give you crisp, scalable designs that look perfect at any size.

SVG vs PNG vs EPS — What's the Difference?

  • SVG — Scalable Vector Graphics. Perfect for cutting machines. Scales to any size without losing quality.
  • PNG — Portable Network Graphics. Great for printing, sublimation and digital use. Fixed resolution.
  • EPS — Encapsulated PostScript. Professional vector format for design software like Illustrator.
  • DXF — Drawing Exchange Format. Used with some cutting machines and CAD software.

The best bundles include all four formats so you have maximum flexibility across projects.

How to Use SVG Bundles with Cricut

  • Open Cricut Design Space and click "New Project"
  • Click "Upload" and select your SVG file
  • Choose "Complex" as the image type for detailed designs
  • Resize and position your design on the canvas
  • Select your material and cut!

How to Use SVG Bundles with Silhouette

Silhouette Studio handles SVG files natively (in Business Edition and above). Simply drag and drop your SVG file into the workspace, resize it, and send to your Silhouette machine.

Making Money with SVG Bundles

Many successful Etsy sellers use SVG bundles to create and sell physical products like custom shirts, mugs, tote bags and home decor. With a commercial license, you can legally sell products made from these designs — making each bundle purchase an investment in your business.
