SVG: shapes you can keep changing

SVG is vector-based, so it scales without losing sharpness. It is often the best starting point for cutting machines, recoloring and simple logo-like graphics.

PNG and JPG: pixels with different jobs

PNG supports transparency and is especially useful for sublimation, overlays and stickers. JPG is compact and reliable for full backgrounds, photography and artwork that does not need a transparent edge.

A coherent collection is less about matching everything—and more about repeating the right decision.

EPS and DXF: production bridges

EPS is a scalable exchange format used in many professional vector workflows. DXF is common in cutting and CAD-adjacent tools. Always check the software you plan to use before choosing the final export.
