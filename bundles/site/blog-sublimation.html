The Ultimate Guide to Sublimation Bundles in 2025

📅 October 2, 2025⏱ 6 min read

Sublimation printing has exploded in popularity among crafters, small business owners and Etsy sellers. The vibrant, permanent colors and professional finish make sublimated products irresistible to buyers. But to succeed with sublimation, you need great designs — and that's where design bundles come in.

What Makes a Great Sublimation Bundle?

Not every digital bundle is created for sublimation. Here's what to look for:

  • PNG format with transparent backgrounds — sublimation printing requires PNG files, not SVG
  • 300 DPI or higher resolution — low-res files will look blurry when printed on tumblers or shirts
  • Commercial license — essential if you're selling your sublimated products
  • Full tumbler wraps — full wrap designs save hours of design time

Sublimation Tips for Beginners

Once you have your design bundle, here are some quick tips to get the best results:

  • Always mirror your image before printing for sublimation transfer
  • Use sublimation-coated blanks — regular mugs and shirts won't work
  • Lint-roll your shirts before applying the transfer
  • Press at the correct temperature and time for each product type
  • Use heat-resistant tape to keep your transfer in place

Why Buy Bundles Instead of Individual Designs?

A single design might cost $3–$10 on its own. A bundle gives you 20–150+ designs for the same price or less. For an Etsy seller or sublimation business, this is a massive cost saving that goes straight to your profit margin.
