Best Font Bundles for Designers & Crafters in 2025

📅 September 28, 2025⏱ 6 min read

Typography is one of the most powerful tools in a designer's arsenal. The right font can make a logo unforgettable, a t-shirt design irresistible, or a social media post stop-scrolling worthy. Font bundles give you access to dozens of premium typefaces at a fraction of the individual cost.

Types of Fonts in Design Bundles

  • Script fonts — Flowing, handwritten-style fonts. Perfect for wedding stationery, logos and elegant designs.
  • Handwritten fonts — Casual, authentic feel. Great for greeting cards, social media and craft projects.
  • Retro/Vintage fonts — 70s, 80s and 90s inspired. Perfect for t-shirt designs and nostalgia branding.
  • Sans-serif fonts — Clean and modern. Ideal for minimalist branding and UI design.
  • Display/Decorative fonts — Eye-catching statement fonts for headlines and posters.

How to Install and Use Font Bundles

Using font bundles is straightforward:

  • Download and unzip the font bundle
  • On Windows: right-click each .ttf or .otf file and choose "Install"
  • On Mac: double-click each font file and click "Install Font"
  • Restart your design software (Canva, Photoshop, Illustrator, Cricut Design Space, etc.)
  • Your new fonts will appear in the font dropdown menu

Using Fonts in Cricut Design Space

Once installed on your computer, custom fonts are automatically available in Cricut Design Space. Simply type your text, select the text layer, and choose your installed font from the font dropdown. For cutting projects, use thicker fonts and weld overlapping letters for clean cuts.

Font Licensing: What You Need to Know
